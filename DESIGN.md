# Visual language

Lora is the primary font throughout, self-hosted in `fonts/` with its license.

| Role | Size | Weight | Color |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Title | 32px | 700 | Near-black #111111 |
| Heading | 24px | 600 | Near-black #111111 |
| Subheading | 18px | 600 | Near-black #111111 |
| Text | 18px | 400 | Charcoal #333333 |
| Subtext | 18px | 400 | Gray #666666 |

White (#FFFFFF) is the primary background. Pastel coral (#F4B8B8) and pastel blue (#BEDDF2) accent timeline entries, tags, and project artwork. Keep text dark on pastel fills. Neutral grays are reserved for secondary text, borders, and placeholders.

`design.css` defines the shared tokens and typography after each page’s layout stylesheet. Responsive layouts adjust spacing and arrangement while preserving the 32/24/18px scale. Home stays within the viewport, with horizontal scrolling inside its timeline.
